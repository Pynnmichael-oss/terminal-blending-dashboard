Entry check only. PTOT and the analyzer's ASTM RVP are either typed by the operator or read from an attached analyzer photo. The application compares the pair and blocks Save/Generate if the values do not correlate. Where both values were read from the same photo the comparison is self-confirming, and the reader's instrument-equation self-check is the meaningful verification — the photo appendix records which basis applied to each level. In an EPA RVP season, the ASTM entry is retained in the data record but the CoA reports the EPA-adjusted RVP calculated from PTOT.
