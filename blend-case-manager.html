Blend RVP target

Terminal blend DVPE target used for butane dosing and margin capture. Set here by Compliance only — operations cannot change it during a blend. Validated against the RVP ceiling in effect for the chosen date; targeting above the ceiling is allowed with a warning so product can be pre-blended to the next volatility level and held for later release.

Per-blend target override

Requires PQ manager approval. The compliance-set terminal target governs every blend by default. On an individual case it can only be increased here, and only with a recorded PQ manager approval and attestation. It cannot be lowered below the compliance default, and it locks once the load plan is confirmed and trucks are ordered. Increases are validated against the RVP ceiling for the effective date; above-ceiling values are allowed with a warning so product can be pre-blended to the next volatility level and held.
